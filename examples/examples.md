# Examples

Three real cases from the author's use, with names and details removed.

**A status message to a team (principle 9).** The draft said "it goes out this week, well before the demo".
The work was not finished. Suggested: "plan is to get it out this week, so it's in well before the demo".
Learn: say "plan to" for a date that still depends on work not done.

**A message to a friend.** It already listened and asked a question. Nothing was returned.

**An email to church leaders (principles 7 and 20).** The draft said "when I write to a few professors"
after those emails had already gone out. Because leaders expect his own words, he got a note and a word-level
edit only: "I've already written to a few professors for advice". Learn: tell your leaders which steps you
have already taken.
